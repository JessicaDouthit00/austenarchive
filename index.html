Regency
England

Jane Austen wrote during a period of profound social transformation — the Napoleonic Wars, the Industrial Revolution's dawn, and a rigid class system that determined the entire trajectory of a woman's life through the institution of marriage.

Her genius lay in excavating this world not through broad strokes, but through the intimate theater of drawing rooms, country estates, and whispered conversations — finding the universe in a teacup.
